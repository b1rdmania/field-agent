## The shape of the week

Nov 17, 2026 (Day 0) is investor-only in the morning at Finlandia Hall and founder-only programming elsewhere in the day, so the floor itself is split by audience before the main event even opens (finlandiatalo.fi, linkedin.com (Slush Day 0 post)). The main conference runs Nov 18-19 at Messukeskus (therecursive.com, vdura.com).

Breakfast before the doors open is a contested slot. Operators & Friends, 10x Founders, and European Women in VC all run breakfast events, and several sources put them on Day 1 morning (linkedin.com (Operators & Friends post), linkedin.com/posts/10x-founders, europeanwomenvc.org). This is a crowded slot.

Evenings are also contested. The Slush Afterparty is the official night event (linkedin.com/posts/slush_slush-afterparty). Multiple private dinners stack against it and each other on the same nights: Nov 19 for the AI x Martech Leaders Dinner (linkedin.com (N.Rich post)) and Nov 20 for the Realm Technologies GTM dinner (linkedin.com (Realm Technologies post)). Nov 20 falls after the main event ends, which the Pomerania Rocks delegation post confirms is when people plan casual post-conference meetups in the city center (linkedin.com (delegation post)).

The research does not describe hour-by-hour keynote or expo-floor timing for Nov 18-19. That gap is marked here, not inferred.

## Where a room fits

**Nov 20 morning, post-conference breakfast or debrief.** The Redpine debrief and the Operators & Friends breakfast pattern both show this slot works once the main two days end (linkedin.com (Redpine post), linkedin.com (Realm Technologies post)). Attendees are still in Helsinki but the conference pressure is off, so a smaller sit-down format fits.

**Day 0, Nov 17, outside the Investor Day and Founder Day blocks.** Investor Day is closed to startups and media, and Founder Day is programmed around specific tracks like Hardware Foundry (finlandiatalo.fi, linkedin.com (Slush Day 0 post)). A narrow, unbranded slot exists for anyone not credentialed into either, but the research does not confirm what fills it. Flag this as a possible gap, not a confirmed one.

**Daytime hours on Nov 18-19, away from breakfast and the Afterparty.** This is the inferred open window from the section above. A short-format meeting or roundtable, not a party, suits it, since it competes with the main stage and expo rather than another side event.

## Logistics that decide it

The main event is at Messukeskus, Helsinki's Expo and Convention Centre (eventzr.com, vdura.com). Day 0 Investor Day runs at Finlandia Hall, a separate venue from Messukeskus (finlandiatalo.fi). The research found no source stating the walking distance or transit time between the two venues.

Several venue options sit in central Helsinki, away from Messukeskus: Ravintola Domus near Kamppi Centre, Kellotorni at Helsinki Central Railway Station, Roba Lounge on Iso Roobertinkatu, Tokoinranta in Hakaniemi, and BUD in Punavuori, which the listing describes as a short walk from the core city center (ravintoladomus.fi, kellotorni.fi, robalounge.fi, hiiohoi.fi, venuu.fi). None of these sources confirms distance to Messukeskus specifically.

Capacity constraints are documented for two of these spaces. Roba Lounge holds standing room for about 50 guests and seating for about 25 (robalounge.fi). BUD holds 40 standing and 32 seated (venuu.fi).

No source in this research gives a price for any venue, ticket, or side event. None is stated here.

## The read

Slush deliberately runs a dense, curated main program, with over 10,000 pre-scheduled meetings across two days and a matchmaking system built to drive founder-investor contact on the floor (eventzr.com). One analyst source states directly that the off-program dinners are where conviction is actually built, even though the main stage sets the tone (shaer.io). That suggests the fringe is not a substitute for the floor at Slush. It is where the floor's matchmaking gets converted into real relationships, through the breakfasts and dinners layered around the two official days.
