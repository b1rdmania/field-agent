## Tiering

**Tier 1, seat at the next event**
- **Revolut**: It is the largest and most technically sophisticated fintech in the set. Its scale alone earns a direct seat.
- **Starling Bank**: It is the closest structural match to Monzo. It is a UK digital bank with comparable headcount. It has high relevance and an easy narrative.
- **Swan**: It builds an API and infra-first product for other banks. Its buyer persona is platform engineering, the same one Exa sells to elsewhere. It is worth an in-person conversation.
- **Aevi**: It has real revenue ($55.6M) and an enterprise customer base of FIs, PSPs, and retailers. This signals budget and technical maturity.

**Tier 2, nurture touch**
- **Vivid Money, Kroo Bank, Snappi**: They fit the neobank category but are smaller or earlier-stage. Keep them warm with content and nurture. Escalate once headcount or funding milestones justify a seat.
- **PAYSTRAX, VIALET**: They are plausible payments infra buyers but have under 150 employees. Monitor for growth signals before escalating.
- **Atrya, Ivy, Ambr**: They are too small or actively shrinking to justify event spend now. Ambr's headcount is down 100% year over year. Ivy's headcount is down 66% year over year. Keep them on low-touch nurture only. Revisit if funding or headcount reverses.

## Gaps

Before any of these enter the programme, the account owner must confirm:

1. **Actual EMEA presence vs. registered HQ**: Several companies, including Vivid, Swan, and Ambr, list workforce spread across non-EMEA countries such as Kazakhstan, the Philippines, and Hong Kong. Confirm the buying team or decision-maker sits in-region.
2. **Live headcount and funding status for the shrinking names**: Ambr has 6 people, down 100% year over year. Ivy has 14 people, down 66% year over year. Both may be winding down or pivoting. Verify they are still operating before any outreach.
3. **Technical buyer identification**: This data does not confirm that any of these companies use, evaluate, or budget for AI search or infra tooling. It needs a named contact or a job-posting signal, such as data, ML, or search roles, before it qualifies as a real pipeline account rather than just a firmographic lookalike.
4. **Revenue/funding data currency**: Several figures are sparse or inconsistent. For example, Vivid shows $5M revenue against EUR175M funding, and Atrya shows $1.8M funding. Confirm these against a second source, such as Crunchbase or LinkedIn, before sizing the opportunity.
5. **Duplicate/overlap check against existing pipeline**: Confirm that none of these companies, especially Revolut and Starling, are already being worked by another rep or region.
