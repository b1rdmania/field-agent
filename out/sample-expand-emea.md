## Tiering

**Tier 1 — seat at the next event**
- **Revolut** — largest, most technically sophisticated fintech in the set; scale alone makes it worth a direct seat.
- **Starling Bank** — closest structural match to Monzo (UK digital bank, comparable headcount); high relevance, easy narrative.
- **Swan** — API/infra-first product for other banks, meaning the buyer persona (platform engineering) is the same one Exa sells to elsewhere; worth an in-person conversation.
- **Aevi** — real revenue ($55.6M) and enterprise customer base (FIs, PSPs, retailers) signal budget and technical maturity.

**Tier 2 — nurture touch**
- **Vivid Money, Kroo Bank, Snappi** — genuine neobank category fit but smaller/earlier-stage; keep warm via content/nurture until headcount or funding milestones justify a seat.
- **PAYSTRAX, VIALET** — plausible payments infra buyers but sub-150 headcount; monitor for growth signals before escalating.
- **Atrya, Ivy, Ambr** — too small or actively shrinking (Ambr -100% YoY, Ivy -66% YoY headcount) to justify event spend now; low-touch nurture only, revisit if funding/headcount reverses.

## Gaps

Before any of these enter the programme, the account owner must confirm:

1. **Actual EMEA presence vs. registered HQ** — several (Vivid, Swan, Ambr) list workforce spread across non-EMEA countries (Kazakhstan, Philippines, Hong Kong); confirm the buying team/decision-maker sits in-region.
2. **Live headcount and funding status for the shrinking names** — Ambr (6 people, -100% YoY) and Ivy (14 people, -66% YoY) look like they may be winding down or pivoting; verify they're still operating before any outreach.
3. **Technical buyer identification** — this data has no confirmation that any of these companies use, evaluate, or budget for AI search/infra tooling; needs a named contact or job-posting signal (data/ML/search roles) before qualifying as a real pipeline account, not just a firmographic lookalike.
4. **Revenue/funding data currency** — several figures (e.g., Vivid's $5M revenue vs. EUR175M funding, Atrya's $1.8M funding) are sparse or inconsistent; confirm against a second source (Crunchbase/LinkedIn) before sizing the opportunity.
5. **Duplicate/overlap check against existing pipeline** — confirm none of these (especially Revolut, Starling) are already being worked by another rep or region.
