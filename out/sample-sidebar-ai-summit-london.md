## The shape of the week

The official agenda runs a tight two-day core on 10-11 June at Tobacco Dock. A training day on 9 June, the AI Skills Accelerator, comes first but is not the main event. The research found one scheduled early slot in the two conference days. It runs 9:30-10:30 on the morning of 10 June, the Future AI Leaders networking session. The summit's own materials call it "start your summit experience." This shows the summit treats the first 90 minutes of day one as a soft-open networking window. It is not dense keynote content. A named session at 10:45 on 10 June, from Tavily and Nebius, confirms stage content runs in parallel by mid-morning.

The summit runs 8 stages and 14 tracks across two days. Expo and stage hours likely run from about 9:30 to 17:00 both days, based on comparable Informa events. This is the contested zone, where attention sits on the floor. Any competing event fights the agenda itself. Evenings are where the fringe events concentrate. The HPE/NVIDIA afterparty, the Graphwise evening, and the Thames cruise are all evening events by nature. The UKTA/AGI Odyssey dinner sits deliberately on the night before the summit opens, 9 June. This suggests organisers see the pre-summit evening as a genuinely open slot. It does not compete with post-summit fatigue.

The research did not find a single dominant closing-night party for the evening of day two, 11 June. No source names a headline closing event for that night. This is a gap, not a confirmed opening. See Gaps.
