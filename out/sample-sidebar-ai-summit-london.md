## The shape of the week

The official agenda is a tight two-day core (10-11 June, Tobacco Dock), preceded by a training day (9 June, AI Skills Accelerator) that is not the main event. Within the two conference days, the research shows one scheduled early slot — 9:30-10:30 on the morning of 10 June, the Future AI Leaders networking session — explicitly positioned as "start your summit experience," which tells us the summit's own programming treats the first 90 minutes of day one as a soft-open networking window rather than dense keynote content. A named session at 10:45 on 10 June (Tavily/Nebius) confirms stage content is running in parallel by mid-morning.

*Inference*: with 8 stages and 14 tracks running across two days, expo and stage hours (roughly 9:30-17:00 both days, based on comparable Informa events) are the contested zone — that's when attention is on the floor and any competing pull is fighting the agenda itself. Evenings are where the fringe concentrates: the HPE/NVIDIA afterparty, the Graphwise evening, and the Thames cruise are all evening-format by nature. The UKTA/AGI Odyssey dinner deliberately sits the night *before* the summit opens (9 June), suggesting organisers see pre-summit evening as a genuinely open slot rather than one competing with post-summit fatigue.

*Inference*: the closing evening of day two (11 June) is not evidenced in the research as having a single dominant "big party" the way some tech weeks do — no source names a headline closing-night event. This is a gap, not a confirmed opening (see Gaps).

- Tobacco Dock sits in Wapping, East London. Studio Spaces (a venue used for adjacent industrial-style events) is described as "minutes from Wapping and Tower Hill tube stations," which anchors the walking geography around the venue.
- Several named fringe/dinner venues are not walking distance from Tobacco Dock: Simmons Bar, The Chamberlain Hotel, Pangura Limited (75 King William Street, EC4N 7BE) and Arros QD/Chotto Matte (Soho) are all central/City London, meaning a west-central dinner requires a deliberate travel decision away from the venue, not a stroll.

## The read

The evidence points to a floor-heavy event: 8 stages, 14 tracks, 300+ speakers and a VisionAIres VIP programme are all built to keep senior attendees inside Tobacco Dock across two dense days, and the one confirmed early-morning slot (9:30) is itself summit-branded, not independent. The fringe that does exist is thin and scattered — a handful of partner dinners and one pre-summit fringe talk — rather than a dense unofficial ecosystem like SF's AI Engineer World's Fair. That said, the pre-summit evening (9 June) shows real but light activity, and no source confirms a single dominant closing-night party, so a fringe play is viable if it's positioned as a genuine complement (dinner, breakfast) rather than a competing draw during expo hours.
