## The read

The set's EMEA presence is thin and format-light compared to what the same companies do in the US. Tavily's US activity (NYU Hackathon, Elastic meetups, LangChain Interrupt sponsorship, Composio dinner) shows a developer-community-and-booth playbook it has only partially exported to Europe — a Munich partnership, a Prague meetup, one Amsterdam exhibit slot. Brave is the most visibly "enterprise-conference" of the set in EMEA, appearing as a paid sponsor at The AI Summit London and MAD//FEST, which reads as a deliberate advertising/privacy-brand play rather than a developer-API go-to-market — consistent with Brave's broader ad-supported business, not just its Search API. Perplexity's single EMEA touch (Billion Pound Build at London Tech Week) is a marquee-moment stunt, not a sustained programme — no other EMEA sponsorship surfaced anywhere in this research. Firecrawl, You.com, and Parallel show zero EMEA footprint in what this research turned up, despite all three running visible US event programmes (Firecrawl's hackathons and TDC São Paulo, You.com's NYC dinner series and Tech Week hosting). That gap is worth taking at face value rather than assuming under-research: it suggests EMEA field marketing in this category is still a secondary motion for most of the set, built opportunistically around whichever flagship week (London Tech Week, MWC-adjacent) is already drawing the buyer.

The white space is broad. No one in the set is running a developer-dinner or small-room executive format in EMEA the way You.com and Tavily do in the US — the "curated dinner" formats that recur across this research (World Summit AI's executive dinner series, IDC & SAP dinners, HTEC's AI First series) are being run by adjacent vendors and analyst firms, not by any search/crawl competitor. Paris (RAISE Summit, VivaTech) and Amsterdam (AI & Big Data Expo, World Summit AI) both show confirmed enterprise-buyer audiences with no competitor from this set present — RAISE Summit in particular looks like an open lane given its NVIDIA/Oracle/OpenAI/Anthropic vendor mix. Berlin/Munich developer-community events are Tavily's territory alone; nobody else in the set shows up there. This is inference from absence, not confirmed intent — it's possible competitors are present at these events in ways this research didn't surface (e.g., as unbadged attendees, not sponsors), so treat the "nobody's there" read as provisional pending a deeper pass.

## Gaps

- This research is built almost entirely from LinkedIn posts and a handful of event-partner pages — it will systematically miss unannounced attendance (badge-scan-only, no sponsor tier, no social post) for every competitor in the set.
- No confirmation either way on whether Firecrawl, You.com, or Parallel attended any EMEA event as an unsponsored attendee rather than a sponsor/host — absence of a marketing post is not proof of absence from the room.
- Exact dates are missing for the Munich {Tech: Europe} event and MAD//FEST London — need direct event-page confirmation.
- The "Parallel" hits in this research (GLOBALS, European Blockchain Convention, CIGRE) could not be confirmed as the search company Parallel vs. name collision with other "Parallel" brands — needs a targeted search on the specific entity (Parallel Web Systems / parallel.ai) plus a check of its own site/LinkedIn for an events page.
- Buyer-audience quality for several EMEA events (HumanX Amsterdam, {Tech: Europe} Munich, On Ring Lisbon) is asserted from the event's own self-description rather than an independent attendee breakdown — worth verifying with past-year attendee lists where available.
- No pricing/scale data on what sponsorship tiers Brave or Tavily bought (booth vs. logo-only vs. speaking slot) — matters for budgeting a comparable Exa presence.
- Nothing here covers 2025 EMEA history, so there's no view on whether this set's EMEA presence is growing, shrinking, or stable year over year — worth a follow-up pull scoped to 2025 for trend context.
