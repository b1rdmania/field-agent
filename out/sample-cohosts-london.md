## Partner read

AI Tinkerers London and AI Demo Night hold the list Exa would want most. Both run monthly or near-monthly, screen for technical attendees, and centre the format on live demos, which is where a search-API company can show a working integration rather than pitch a slide. AI Builders London is a close second. It draws a full room during London Tech Week and states its audience as developers, founders and career switchers with working AI knowledge. GDG London's Build with AI series adds reach through Google's developer network, though the source ties it closely to Gemini and Vertex AI, so a competing model provider slot may sit awkwardly there. This is inference: no source states GDG London's sponsor terms.

Demo nights and hackathons fit a partner slot well, because most already run as a stage for outside builders to show what they made, and a sponsor can supply API credits or a technical track without taking over the room. The AI Summit London and the London AI Startup Conference sit at the other end. The Summit already runs sponsor tiers with named companies such as EY, CoreWeave and Weights & Biases holding booths, so a slot there is a paid sponsorship, not a co-host role. The London AI Startup Conference caps attendance at 120 and is already backed by Google Cloud, Zendesk and Stripe, which reads as a partner slate that is likely full. This is inference: the source does not state whether further sponsors are being sought.
